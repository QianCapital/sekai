# Code of Conduct

sekai has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone participating in the project — in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space — is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help keep the project a
welcoming place for people of every background and level of experience. Harassment and
personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **opensource@qiancapital.com**.
All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow
the code of conduct may face consequences determined by the other project leaders.
